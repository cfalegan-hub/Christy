# UK Tax Reference Notes

**These are general reference points for orientation only — not tax advice.**
Rates, allowances, and thresholds change yearly (usually each 6 April) and
depend on your specific circumstances. Confirm current figures with HMRC or
a qualified accountant/tax adviser before relying on them, especially near
filing deadlines.

## Key dates (general pattern, confirm each year)

- **UK tax year:** 6 April to 5 April the following year (not calendar
  year — see open question in `docs/open-questions.md`).
- **Self Assessment registration deadline:** 5 October following the end of
  the tax year in which you need to file for the first time.
- **Paper return deadline:** 31 October following the tax year end.
- **Online return deadline:** 31 January following the tax year end.
- **Balancing payment / first payment on account due:** 31 January
  following the tax year end.
- **Second payment on account due:** 31 July.
